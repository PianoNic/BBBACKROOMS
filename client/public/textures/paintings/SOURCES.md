# Painting texture sources

The images in this folder are community meme images that were collected from a
subreddit frequented by students of a school in Baden. They are used in-game
as wall art hung around the world, in the spirit of the memes that
circulate among the community the game is inspired by.

These images are reproduced here for a non-commercial fan project and are not
used for any commercial purpose. A number of originally considered images
were withheld from this set because they showed identifiable people or
institutional marks, and are not included here.

If you are depicted in one of these images, or otherwise believe an image
should be removed, please contact kontakt@backrooms-baden.ch and it will be
taken down.
